\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Partition draws are a first-order source of uncertainty in federated LoRA evaluations at Dirichlet $\alpha{=}0.1$. On TinyLlama they account for about half of held-out-loss variance alongside a comparable interaction share; on LLaMA-3.2-3B the interaction dominates and the partition main-effect interval includes zero. Rankings of near-tied methods flip under few draws, while gaps an order of magnitude larger than the per-draw paired SD do not. Detecting the observed FedIT--FLoRA near-tie would require hundreds of paired partitions at 1.1B and dozens at 3B. Evaluations should therefore report how many partition draws were used, pair methods on the same draws, and state the smallest difference the design can detect. We make no method recommendation.
